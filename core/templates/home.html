Our Mission

To build a dedicated blood donor network within the Mar Koorilose Bava Church community, bringing together our members and families who are willing to donate blood and support fellow community members during medical emergencies.

Our mission is to connect community donors with patients from our community who require blood, particularly those travelling from Kunnamkulam to Ernakulam for specialised medical treatment.

Through the Youth League, we aim to create a trusted and responsive support system where our community stands together in times of medical need, ensuring that no member of our community faces a blood shortage alone.

💡

Our Vision

To create a strong, united, and dependable blood donor community within Mar Koorilose Bava Church, where every eligible donor is ready to support a fellow community member in need.

We envision a community where the bond between our members extends beyond generations and geographical boundaries—from Kunnamkulam to Ernakulam—and where voluntary blood donation becomes a lasting expression of our faith, compassion, and responsibility towards one another.

Our vision is to make our community a reliable lifeline for our own people, whenever and wherever they need us.
